# Product Vision: Bundesliga Wrapped

## 1. Product Overview

**Bundesliga Wrapped** is a personalized, cinematic football recap experience that transforms raw match data into emotionally resonant, shareable fan stories. Inspired by Spotify Wrapped and Netflix Year in Review, it creates a premium, mobile-first experience that makes Bundesliga fans feel seen, understood, and excited to share their football journey.

### Core Concept
Each fan receives a unique, AI-generated recap that tells their story of the season - the emotional highs and lows, the unforgettable moments, the club loyalty, and the fan identity they've built through a season of Bundesliga football.

---

## 2. User Problem

### Current Gaps in Football Engagement

**Problem 1: Data Overload, No Storytelling**
Fans have access to mountains of football data but no way to make sense of it emotionally. Stats sites show numbers but don't connect them to the fan experience.

**Problem 2: No Personalized Recap**
Unlike music streaming services, football lacks personalized season recaps. Fans can't see "their season" summarized in a meaningful way.

**Problem 3: Social Media Fragmentation**
Match moments are scattered across Twitter, Instagram, and fan groups. No platform aggregates and personalizes these moments for the fan.

**Problem 4: Passive Consumption**
Fans consume content but don't feel like active participants in the story of their season.
