## Persona Design Guidelines

### For Hardcore Fans
- **Depth over simplicity:** Provide detailed analysis
- **Tradition focus:** Highlight club history and legacy
- **Emotional intensity:** Don't shy away from strong emotions
- **Community features:** Enable fan-to-fan connections

### For Casual Fans
- **Simplicity first:** Easy-to-understand narratives
- **Entertainment focus:** Make it fun and engaging
- **Visual storytelling:** Use strong imagery and animations
- **Accessibility:** Explain terms and traditions

### For Fantasy Users
- **Data depth:** Provide detailed statistics
- **Predictive insights:** Help with future decisions
- **Comparative analysis:** Enable player/team comparisons
- **Trend identification:** Highlight patterns and trends

### For Social Creators
- **Shareability:** Optimize for Instagram, TikTok, Twitter
- **Speed:** Quick content creation (under 5 minutes)
- **Trending:** Align with current social trends
- **Customization:** Enable personal branding
